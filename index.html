01 / ABOUT

From spatial data to better decisions.

I am a PhD researcher in Civil Engineering at the University of Auckland. My research examines how changing geospatial information can help assess transportation infrastructure resilience during natural disasters.

My background combines transport engineering, geoinformatics and software development. I work across spatial databases, remote sensing, computer vision and web mapping.
